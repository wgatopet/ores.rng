# ores.rng ## What is ores.rng? - *ores.rng* is a game based on TurboWarp created by "wgatopet". it is based on the Roblox game "REx:Reincarnated" - the main part of the game is about rolling for ores of varying rarities, from 1/1 to chances in the billions ## How to play: - to play, you press the roll button, over and over and over again, until you get something rare! **yep, that’s the entire gameplay.** - every 100 rolls, there is a 25x luck boost for one roll, and every 10k rolls, there is a 2500x luck boost for one roll. this enables you to *potentially* get rarer things - the index tells you *how rare an ore is*, *how many of them you have*, and *the* *last time you rolled a selected ore*. - you can save your progress anytime using the built in save system, meaning you don’t have to worry about your progress being wiped when you leave, like scratch projects do
